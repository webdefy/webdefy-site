How it runs

Priced by phase, not by the hour.

Three phases, each with a fixed quote agreed after one scoping call.

  1. Phase 1 · fixed quote

    Assess

    Find the one workflow worth handing to AI.

    • Walk-through of the work as it runs today
    • Data, risk and compliance map
    • Shortlist of use cases, ranked by value and risk
    • Rollout plan with a go or no-go

    Ends with: a plan your risk team has read.

  2. Phase 2 · fixed quote

    Pilot

    Prove it on real work, with a person approving every output.

    • Agent or RAG build on your own data
    • MCP connections to the tools you already use
    • Eval suite written before launch
    • Human review on every output

    Ends with: a pilot your team uses every day.

  3. Phase 3 · fixed quote

    Run and govern

    Keep it safe and useful once real users depend on it.

    • Monitoring and evals on every change
    • Audit trail and access controls
    • Model and prompt updates, tested first
    • Monthly review with your team

    Ends with: AI you can show a regulator.
