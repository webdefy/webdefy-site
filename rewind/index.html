Rewind · by Webdefy

The internet you grew up with, one year at a time.

Pick your birth year and Rewind walks you through the moments that shaped your internet, with your age on every card. Then it turns what you loved into one good thing to do this week, and it ends. No feed, no scrolling.

  • Look back: your years, the moments you remember, hearted in one tap.
  • Notice what was good: what went well this week, built from what you actually did.
  • Do one thing: a plan with people, from your own past. Say it or type it.
